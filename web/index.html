AI-first: how it works

Anyone can describe security in plain language. Olopa turns that into scoped assets, enforceable policies, optimal monitoring, and live risk scores.

  • Verbal policy — Describe what you want (“No dev can read customer data from prod”; “Block uploads to personal cloud”). No YAML, no Rego by hand.
  • Scope resources & assets — We discover and map your resources and assets across network and devices so policies are grounded in what you actually have.
  • Generate policies to mitigate threats — From your intent and asset scope, we generate enforceable policies that mitigate the threats you care about.
  • Optimal monitoring — We recommend what to monitor and where—not “collect everything”—so you get signal without noise or cost.
  • User behavioral analytics → real-time risk score — Behavior across users and endpoints feeds a live risk score so you see who and what is risky, in real time.

Why Olopa

Security tools are either noisy, slow, or require experts to write rules. We combine AI-first policy (describe in words) with kernel-level visibility and enforcement—one agent, observe first, enforce when ready.
