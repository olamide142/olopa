How Olopa + AACE Work

You state your intent in words. Olopa maps your infrastructure, generates controls, and—through AACE—adapts permissions and risk in real time based on live behavior.
No YAML. No static rules. Just protections that adjust as your team and environment change.

  • Conversational intent → Policy — Say what you want (“No dev can read prod data”, “Alert on sensitive exfil”). Olopa turns it into adaptive policy.
  • Live asset discovery — Olopa builds a security map: users, devices, data, networks, for precise controls.
  • Policy + asset graph → Adaptive enforcement — Each policy is enforced at the right point—identity, kernel, cloud—moment by moment.
  • Behavioral monitoring: AACE in action — AACE learns live behavior for each identity/device, adjusting permissions continuously.
  • Real-time trust & risk scoring — Every user and device gets a dynamic trust score. Security responds instantly.
AACE isn’t just “behavior analytics”. It’s a novel, always-on control loop—like adaptive autopilot for your security.
