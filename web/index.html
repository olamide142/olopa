What Olopa does
Olopa connects multiple security layers that are usually disconnected, so risk can be evaluated as a full chain instead of isolated events.

That means your team can answer:
  • What happened?
  • Why was it risky?
  • What action should policy take next?
All with operator review and control over when stricter enforcement is enabled.
Need static controls too? Olopa supports traditional static policy enforcement alongside adaptive decisioning.

How Teams Roll Out Olopa

  • Define policy intent
    Capture the outcomes you care about in clear policy terms, from classic controls to AI-agent specific guardrails.
  • Observe and evaluate
    Olopa continuously evaluates live activity and maps it to policy decisions without forcing broad disruption.
  • Review confidence
    Security teams review outcomes, tune posture, and verify decisions before scaling enforcement.
  • Enforce in stages
    Turn on stronger controls where trust is highest, while keeping sensitive or uncertain paths under review.
